## Done

- **Lessons and practice**: lesson pages; typed answers of seven kinds with specific feedback, hints, retry, reveal and model answers; a "See the example" button on questions that refer to an example, table or method in their lesson; provenance on every item ("Your book · p.12", "Study Hub").
- **Welcome and profile**: a first visit asks for age, school level and year (suggested from the age) and an optional nickname; Settings › About you changes them, confirming a new year. The e2e suite answers the Welcome page once in global setup and starts every other test from that saved storage.
- **Levels and extras**: each learner sees their own level (University, or every year of primary or secondary school with their own year first) and the extras they chose; guests see their level only, with a note on signing in. Signed-in learners (and local mode) are asked once what they want to study and change it in Settings › Courses I want to see; a hidden course opened by its link offers to show its area again. Certifications follow the extras and leave the rail without them. School courses (`elementary`, `secondary`) are open to guests and checked by the build against `school.yaml` for their year and language; bilingual subjects switch language on the course page. Multiple-choice questions can carry an easy, medium or hard `tier`, picked per subject on its Practice page.
- **Streak page**: `/streak`, linked from Today, with days in a row, the longest run, whether today counts, and twelve weeks of days as bubbles; for guests, signed-in learners and local mode.
- **UASA papers**: Bahasa Melayu Tingkatan 1 (`bm-f1`, Form 1, BM) holds four UASA papers transcribed from the PDFs provided: Kertas 1 and Kertas 2 of 2022/2023 and 2023/2024. Multiple-choice questions are lettered A to D and marked automatically; spelling, grammar, sentence-pattern and *bandingan semacam* answers are typed and checked against the scheme; comprehension, novel, summary and composition answers are marked by the learner with the scheme's bands. Pictures are cropped from the papers; passages, pantun and diagrams are typed. Scores are out of the printed 60 and 40 marks. Kertas 2 of 2023/2024 came without a scheme, so its compositions use the 2022/2023 Kertas 2 bands (the course says so).
- **Sains Tingkatan 1** (`sains-f1`, Form 1, BM, private): revision notes and practice for all nine chapters of the KSSM textbook (35 lessons), each chapter's *Praktis Sumatif* as a mock exam, and two UASA-format papers: UASA 2022/2023 (Set 1, 100 marks, marked by its scheme) and a teachers' *Latihan Akhir Sesi* (Set 2, 98 marks as printed), which came without answers, so its answers are worked out from the textbook pages it cites and say so. Answers the textbook does not give are written from the textbook and marked as such. Where the Set 1 scheme contradicts the textbook (the gas at the cathode), the question follows the textbook and explains why. On a BM paper the one-line answer box also reads in BM ("Taip jawapan anda").
- **Learning model**: FSRS reviews, mastery per topic and skill, due reviews, weaknesses, recent mistakes, saved questions, mixed quizzes; JLPT N5–N1 mock exams with booklet, answer sheet, audio, section timers and raw-score results. An unfinished sitting is kept in the browser and can be resumed after a reload.
- **JLPT**: official-format sample tests N5–N1 (N2 and N1 with their combined language-knowledge & reading section). N1 listening plays each question's own part of the section recordings; in N2 only 問題5 does, and 問題1–4 play the whole section (see below); 新にほんご500問 N4–N5, N3, N2 and N1, all 2,000 questions, every answer either confirmed on the answer page by the importer or checked by eye against the page (55 unreadable answers filled in, 87 unconfirmed ones checked, one OCR misread corrected).
- **HSK**: HSK 1–6 (4,865 words in 146 lessons; every left-out row and every reading that disagrees with CC-CEDICT checked against the page images and corrected or confirmed; two misprints in the HSK 5 book corrected to the standard reading, with a note; phase 2 OCR of 2,842 pages complete).
- **Other content**: Python fundamentals (stage 1 of the AI roadmap), Databases & SQL (4 lessons), 33 certifications checked on 2026-10-11, and JLPT N5–N1 and HSK 1–6 added on 2026-10-14 from jlpt.jp and chinesetest.cn (fees, HSK 5–6 pass marks and the move to HSK 3.0 are marked unverified).
- **University** (Sem 1 2026, UNITEN; private courses, written from your materials):

  | Course | Lessons | Questions | Written from |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | CMPD103 Statistics for Computer Science | 8 | 96 | Chapter 1–6 notes and slides, and your Chapter 1–2 summary; past papers: Tests 1 and 2 (2025/2026) and four finals (2017/18, 2018/19, 2019/20, 2021/22) |
  | CMPD203 Web Programming | 7 | 105 | Chapter 1–6 notes (Chapters 5–6: PHP basics and PHP for form data) |
  | CMPD2123 Machine Learning and Data Analytics | 6 | 64 | Chapter 1–3 slides, including 5 Python exercises; past paper: the CMPD383 2023/24 final (this subject's earlier code) |

  Every exercise with a runnable solution passes `content:verify`. Each course's `contentNotice` lists the slide errors its notes correct. Past papers sit in each subject's **Past papers** section, grouped into midterms, tests and finals; every question can be answered and is marked (drawings are self-reviewed against a model answer). The papers have no answer keys, so the answers are worked by Study Hub, and each ambiguous question accepts every defensible answer with an explanation. Group assignments are left out, and the CMPD203 exercise sheets are not used.
- **Navigation**: the rail lists Today, Courses, Practice, Calendar and Certifications, plus Playground once a course with code exercises is started or on Today. Courses opens on its areas (University, Japanese, Chinese, and the AI engineering roadmap) and shows one area at a time; Certifications opens on its areas (AI and ML, cloud, security, networking, Linux, IT fundamentals, Japanese, Chinese, and the IT courses) the same way.
- **Reset a chapter**: on a course page, a chapter with progress can be started again (after a confirmation): its lessons' progress is cleared and their questions leave the review schedule; the answer history and topic mastery stay.
- **Practice**: starts by choosing a subject (those started or with anything waiting, most due first), then shows that subject's sessions with their counts (due reviews, weaknesses, recent mistakes, saved questions, a mixed quiz), its weak spots and its knowledge by skill. Sessions opened from a subject cover only that subject.
- **Subjects on Today**: switch "Show on Today" on for the subjects you are taking (course page or Settings); Today's reviews and weak topics, the due count and due reviews then cover only those, and other subjects wait quietly. Resume always shows the latest lesson. Today also lists today's classes and dates from the calendar, each linked to its course; a class or date can be linked to any course, not only University subjects.
- **Learners**: several learners per device and per account (siblings on one computer), each with their own progress, calendar, settings and unfinished exam sittings; "Switch learner" on Today, rename and remove in Settings. Guests keep their progress on the device (it survives reloads); after the first sign-in, a device with progress can bring every learner into an empty account, once. Unfinished mock-exam sittings are saved with the learner's data, so they sync to the account.
- **Calendar** (optional; replaced the notebook): weekly timetable per semester (lectures and labs) with teaching-week numbers and clash warnings, and important dates (quiz, test, midterm, final, assignment, extra or make-up class, holiday or break, optionally over several days with the longer event shown first) in week, month and list views; the next 21 days show on Today. Learners can add a semester that has no materials yet (Calendar or the catalog), with optional dates, and remove one they added together with its classes and dates. Works for guests, signed-in learners and local mode. Notes from the old notebook stay in the browser until downloaded or deleted in Settings.
- **Playground, certifications, settings, backups** (backup format version 2 adds the calendar; version 1 files still import), light and dark themes, keyboard use, mobile layout.
- **Storage**: local (IndexedDB) by default; Supabase schema, row level security, repository and sign-in ready (see [SUPABASE.md](SUPABASE.md)).
- **Hosting** (see [DEPLOY.md](DEPLOY.md)): Netlify config and a deploy from this computer (`npm run deploy`, guarded by `npm run check:deploy`). Sign-in with Google on a `/sign-in` page (Discord, GitHub or email can be switched on with `VITE_AUTH_PROVIDERS`); guests can open the University and school courses, see only their own level, and keep their progress on their device; signing in (any account) opens every course and saves progress, and a Netlify edge function refuses the other courses' files, page images, audio and search index to anyone not signed in. Profiles keep each user's name, picture, email and providers.
